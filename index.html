Clinical Synergy

Why Dentistry & Neurosurgery Under One Roof?

Many chronic head, face, and jaw conditions originate at the intricate intersection between cranial nerves and dental occlusion. Our co-located disciplines solve diagnostic dilemmas that standalone centres miss.

Schedule Joint Consult
Condition 01

Trigeminal Neuralgia vs Toothache

Severe facial electric pain is frequently misdiagnosed as routine dental pulp infection. Our combined evaluation distinguishes nerve compression from dental abscesses, preventing unnecessary tooth extractions.

Condition 02

TMJ Dysfunction & Chronic Migraines

Jaw clenching (bruxism) and temporomandibular joint misalignment irritate the trigeminal cervical nucleus, triggering chronic migraines. We treat the bite and nerve pathways in unison.

Condition 03

Craniofacial & Maxillary Trauma

Accidents involving head trauma frequently fracture both the facial skeleton and dental structures. Having an MCh Neurosurgeon and Dental Surgeon on-site guarantees seamless cranial and oral triage.
